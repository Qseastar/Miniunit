# Verification quality gate troubleshooting

- **Schema / concept / scorer / answer**: repair the staged data contract; do not add a fallback scorer or silently skip the template.
- **Source / path**: reconcile chunk ID, file name, and physical pages; do not substitute general knowledge or a PDF screenshot.
- **Position / intent**: check semantic choice IDs and reviewed eligibility; do not score by display index.
- **Isolation / history / evidence**: keep candidate data out of production services and reject tampered sessions; do not bypass validation.
- **UI / CI**: use stable choice IDs internally and text for display. CI has no private PDF or `.env`; do not add either.
- **All sources are context-only**: add a non-context, primary-concept evidence page; a heading page cannot anchor the claim.
- **Topic mismatch**: do not relabel a mismatched evidence page as `context_only`; find a direct primary source or keep the candidate blocked.
- **IDDFS p.53/p.58 chain**: p.53 may be context-only because it establishes the section; p.58 remains the non-context primary evidence page for the properties claim.
