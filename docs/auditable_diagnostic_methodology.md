# Auditable diagnostic methodology

LLM understanding may guide teaching, but it does not directly grant mastery. Mastery evidence comes only from human-reviewed controlled templates, deterministic scorers, validated sessions, and the evidence gate. Candidate templates remain staged until source review and owner approval; source traceability and answer-position audits expose narrow evidence boundaries. Assistance provenance prevents hints or reveals from being presented as independent mastery. A template demonstrates one reviewed capability slice, not complete understanding of a concept.
